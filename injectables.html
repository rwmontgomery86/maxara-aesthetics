Safety First

Medical Care,
Spa Comfort

All injectable treatments at Maxará Aesthetics are administered by a licensed nurse practitioner following medically established protocols. A consultation is always required first — to confirm you're a good candidate, review your health history, and design a plan that's right for you.

Injectables May Not Be Right For You If

  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • You have certain neuromuscular conditions
  • You have an active infection at the treatment site
  • You've had allergic reactions to injectable products

After Your Treatment

  • Stay upright for 4 hours after wrinkle relaxers
  • Skip strenuous exercise for 24 hours
  • Avoid rubbing or massaging treated areas
  • Minor redness or bruising resolves in a few days

FAQ

Know Before
You Book

Everything you need to feel confident before your first injectable appointment.

Wrinkle relaxers are purified neuromodulator injections — at Maxará we use Dysport® — that temporarily soften the repetitive muscle movements behind expression lines — frown lines, forehead creases, and crow's feet. Treatments take just minutes, and results appear gradually over the following days for a rested, natural look.

Dermal fillers are smooth hyaluronic acid gels — at Maxará we use Restylane® and Revanesse® Versa™ — that restore lost volume and refine contours — most commonly in the lips, cheeks, and lower face. Results are visible right away and are designed to enhance your features, never change them.

Sculptra® is a collagen stimulator rather than a traditional filler. Instead of adding volume all at once, it helps your skin gradually rebuild its own collagen over a short series of treatments spaced several weeks apart. Results develop over a few months and can last two years or more.

That's the entire philosophy of our injectable practice. Your nurse practitioner maps your facial anatomy and uses a conservative, build-gradually approach, so you look like yourself on your best day — refreshed, never frozen or overfilled.

Most clients describe a quick pinch. We use ultra-fine needles, and numbing options are available for filler appointments. Treatments are brief — wrinkle relaxer appointments typically take 15 to 30 minutes.

Wrinkle relaxer results typically last 3 to 4 months, while most dermal fillers last 6 to 18 months depending on the product and treatment area, and Sculptra results can last two years or more. At your consultation we'll map out a maintenance plan that fits your goals and budget.

Very little. You can return to most normal activities immediately. Minor redness, swelling, or bruising at injection sites can occur and typically resolves within a few days. We'll send you home with simple aftercare guidance.
