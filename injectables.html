Neurotoxins

Soften The Lines
Expression Leaves

We use Dysport®, a purified neurotoxin that temporarily relaxes the small muscles that cause repeated expression lines. The lines smooth out, and your face still moves naturally.

Frown Lines

The vertical "elevens" between the brows soften and smooth, easing the tired or stern look they can create over time.

Forehead Lines

Relaxes horizontal forehead creases while keeping your brows' natural movement.

Crow's Feet

Softens the fine lines at the outer corners of your eyes without changing your smile.

Results appear over 3–14 days · Typically last 3–4 months · Appointments from 15 minutes

Lips

Cheeks & Midface

Chin & Jawline

Timing Your Treatment

Plan Around
Your Injections

Injectables need about two weeks to settle. If you have skin treatments or a massage on the calendar, here's how to space them out.

  1. 1

    Before

    Come In Hydrated

    Lindsay likes to work with well-hydrated skin, so a HydraFacial before your injections is a nice extra. Recommended, not required.

    Had a chemical peel, IPL, or anything else your skin needs to heal from? Wait until it's fully healed before your injection appointment.

  2. 2

    Day Of

    Your Appointment

    Lindsay goes over your goals, health history, and plan at the start of your visit, then gets started. Neurotoxin appointments usually take 15 to 30 minutes.

  3. 3

    Days 1–14

    Hands Off Your Face

    Skip facials, HydraFacials, peels, microneedling, laser, and IPL. Hold off on massages where you lie face down, too. The face cradle presses right on treated areas while they're still settling.

  4. 4

    Day 14 On

    Back To Your Routine

    Your results have settled, and you're clear to book facials, skin treatments, and massages again.

Safety First

Medical Care,
Spa Comfort

All injectable treatments at Maxará Aesthetics are administered by a licensed nurse practitioner following medically established protocols. At the start of every appointment, Lindsay reviews your health history, confirms you're a good candidate, and talks through the plan with you.

Injectables May Not Be Right For You If

  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • You have certain neuromuscular conditions
  • You have an active infection at the treatment site
  • You've had allergic reactions to injectable products

After Your Treatment

  • Stay upright for 4 hours after neurotoxins
  • Skip strenuous exercise for 24 hours
  • Avoid rubbing or massaging treated areas
  • Minor redness or bruising resolves in a few days

FAQ

Know Before
You Book

Everything you need to feel confident before your first injectable appointment.

Neurotoxins are purified injections (at Maxará we use Dysport®) that temporarily soften the repeated muscle movements behind expression lines such as frown lines, forehead creases, and crow's feet. The injections take just minutes, and results appear gradually over the following days.

Dermal fillers are smooth hyaluronic acid gels (at Maxará we use Restylane® and Revanesse® Versa™) that restore lost volume and shape contours, most commonly in the lips, cheeks, and lower face. Results are visible right away, and the aim is to keep you looking like yourself.

Sculptra® is a collagen stimulator rather than a traditional filler. Instead of adding volume all at once, it helps your skin gradually rebuild its own collagen over a short series of treatments spaced several weeks apart. Results develop over a few months and can last two years or more.
